REQUIREMENT 1: DATASET SELECTION & TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
  • Dataset Metadata: CIFAR-10
    Source: tf.keras.datasets.cifar10
    Domain: General Object Recognition
    Total Samples: 60,000 images
  • Technical Specifications:
    Original Res: 32×32 | Resized Res: 128×128
    Channels: RGB (3) | Classes: 10
  • Data Preprocessing & Augmentation:
    Resize via tf.image.resize | MobileNet preprocess_input [-1, 1]
    Augmentation (Train only): Random Horizontal Flip, Random Rotation ±15%, Random Zoom ±10%, Random Contrast ±10%
  • Data Splitting:
    Train: 6000 imgs (75%)
    Val: 1000 imgs (12.5%)
    Test: 1000 imgs (12.5%)

Conclusion & 4 Key Insights:
  1. App.2 (E2E DL) achieves higher accuracy by adapting backbone features to CIFAR-10 via fine-tuning. App.1 (SVM) is only ~1-3pp behind with 100× faster training.
  2. App.1 advantages: trains in seconds, no GPU required, low overfitting. Limitation: fixed ImageNet features.
    App.2 advantages: task-specific features, higher ceiling. Limitation: requires GPU, longer training.
  3. Time efficiency: App.1 is dramatically faster at 11.8 seconds vs ~20 minutes.
  4. Deployment Strategy: Mobile/Edge → App.1 (no GPU, fast inference). Cloud/GPU → App.2 (maximum accuracy).
